CareConnect Medical Disclaimer

1. Informational Purpose Only: The CareConnect portal and the CareBot AI assistant provide educational and informational resources. They are not intended as clinical diagnostic engines or substitutes for direct physician evaluation.

2. Emergency Triage: If you are experiencing symptoms of a medical emergency — including acute chest pressure, stroke symptoms, uncontrolled bleeding, or respiratory arrest — immediately call 911 or visit the nearest emergency facility.

3. Demonstration Records: In this MVP release by Team TECH NOVA, healthcare provider records and facility profiles are realistic curated demonstration data to evaluate scheduling workflows safely.

CareConnect Privacy Policy

Patient Data Privacy: CareConnect is designed with patient confidentiality at its core. User appointments and medication schedules are strictly isolated to authenticated user accounts.

Minimal Data Collection: In accordance with healthcare best practices, we collect only necessary scheduling data and do not solicit sensitive, unnecessary medical records.

Local Control: Demonstration session data is maintained securely on your client browser, allowing full user control to update or reset records at any time.
